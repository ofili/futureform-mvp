# Healthcare & Telemedicine Sector - Adaptation Strategy

## Context: Sub-Saharan Africa
Healthcare in SSA is characterized by a shortage of doctors (1:10,000+ in some areas), heavy reliance on Community Health Workers (CHWs), and infrastructure challenges (power, connectivity). Digital health (mHealth) is a critical bridge.

## Critical Sub-Dimensions & Question Scoping
Unlike other sectors, **Clinical Safety** and **Patient Privacy** are non-negotiable and require deep assessment.

### L1: Reliability (Est. 15 questions)
*Focus: Life-critical uptime and offline capability.*
- **System Uptime**: Telemedicine platform availability (24/7?).
- **Offline Capability**: Can CHWs collect data/diagnose without internet? (Critical).
- **Data Sync**: Conflict resolution when syncing patient records after offline mode.
- **Device Compatibility**: Performance on low-end smartphones used by CHWs.
- **Emergency Protocols**: What happens if the system fails during an emergency?

### L2: Transparency (Est. 12 questions)
*Focus: Explaining medical logic to users.*
- **Algorithm Transparency**: How does the symptom checker work?
- **Pricing Transparency**: Clear costs for consultation/drugs.
- **Data Usage**: Who sees my HIV/health status? (Stigma risk).
- **Credentials**: Is the doctor on the other end real and licensed?

### L3: Governance (Est. 14 questions)
*Focus: Medical regulation and data protection.*
- **Medical Licensing**: Verification of doctor credentials with local boards.
- **Telemedicine Compliance**: Adherence to national telemedicine guidelines.
- **Patient Data Privacy**: HIPAA/GDPR equivalent compliance (stricter than finance).
- **Clinical Governance**: Medical protocols and oversight committees.

### L4: Competence (Est. 16 questions)
*Focus: Clinical skills and digital literacy.*
- **CHW Training**: Training on both the app and the medical protocols.
- **Doctor Training**: Tele-consultation etiquette and limitations.
- **Patient Literacy**: Can patients understand the advice? (Local languages/voice).
- **Support**: Clinical support desk (not just IT support).

### L5: Integrity (Est. 15 questions)
*Focus: Clinical safety and ethics.*
- **Clinical Safety**: Evidence that the solution doesn't harm patients.
- **Drug Quality**: Preventing counterfeit medicines in the supply chain.
- **Algorithm Bias**: Ensuring diagnostics work for African populations (skin tone, genetics).
- **Ethical Marketing**: Not overselling cures.

### L6: Ecosystem (Est. 18 questions)
*Focus: Integration with the health system.*
- **Referral Pathways**: Connection to physical clinics/hospitals.
- **Supply Chain**: Drug delivery logistics.
- **Health Insurance**: Integration with national/private insurance.
- **Public Health Reporting**: Sharing disease outbreak data with Ministry of Health.

**Total Estimated Questions**: ~90 (Driven by clinical safety needs)

## Key Differences from Other Sectors
1.  **Clinical Risk**: "Move fast and break things" kills people here. Safety > Speed.
2.  **Intermediation**: Most users interact via a CHW, not directly.
3.  **Privacy**: Health data leaks can lead to social ostracization (e.g., HIV status).
4.  **Regulation**: heavily regulated by medical boards, not just tech regulators.
