## Pricing Philosophy Section

### How FutureForm Pricing Works

**Two credit types. One integrated platform.**

**Respondent Credits (RC)**  
Pay for human intelligence — the assessments, interviews, and verification evidence collected from people in your organization or partner ecosystem.

- Used for: Initial assessments, stakeholder interviews, document collection
- Covers: Unlimited questions per respondent, all evidence uploads, AI validation
- Pricing: Per respondent (not per question or document)
- When: Assessment and verification phases

**Evidence Credits (EC)**  
Pay for machine intelligence — the digital signals, API integrations, and automated monitoring that verify claims and track trust over time.

- Used for: Continuous monitoring, IoT data, API integrations, spot-check verification
- Covers: Real-time operational data, government database checks, Local Lite Validator visits
- Pricing: Per evidence type (transparent, not "mystery credits")
- When: Ongoing monitoring and verification

**Most customers start with RC for assessment, then add EC for continuous monitoring.**

## Pricing FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: What happens to unused Respondent Credits?**  
A: RC can be rolled over for up to 12 months on annual plans. Contact your success manager for specific terms.

**Q: What happens to unused Evidence Credits?**  
A: EC can be rolled over for up to 6 months on monitoring packs, 12 months on portfolio pools. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom terms.

**Q: Can I mix and match packs?**  
A: Absolutely. Most customers start with a Guided Pack (RC) for assessment, then add a Monitoring Pack (EC) for ongoing verification.

**Q: Do you offer volume discounts?**  
A: Yes. Enterprise customers receive discounted RC after initial 100 credits. Portfolio pools include built-in volume discounts (up to 60% off base EC pricing).

**Q: Can I pay monthly instead of upfront?**  
A: Monthly payment plans available for Enterprise and Portfolio customers on annual commitments.

---

### Respondent Credit Questions

**Q: What counts as a "respondent"?**  
A: Any individual who completes assessment questions or submits verification evidence. One person = one RC, regardless of how many questions they answer or documents they upload.

**Q: Can the same person be a respondent in multiple assessments?**  
A: Yes, but each assessment consumes RC. If the same CEO participates in 3 assessments, that's 3 RC (one per assessment).

**Q: What if I need more respondents than my pack includes?**  
A: You can purchase additional RC at your tier's rate. Enterprise customers get volume discounts automatically.

**Q: Do Local Lite Validators count as respondents?**  
A: No. LLV evidence collection is charged via Evidence Credits, not Respondent Credits.

---

### Evidence Credit Questions

**Q: How do I know how many EC I'll need?**  
A: Our team helps you estimate based on your monitoring needs. Common benchmark: 2,500 EC covers 6-12 months of basic monitoring for one company.

**Q: Can I use EC for multiple companies?**  
A: Yes. EC pools work across your entire portfolio. You're not locked to specific companies.

**Q: What if I run out of EC?**  
A: You can top up anytime. Enterprise customers can enable auto-reload when balance drops below a threshold.

**Q: Do EC expire?**  
A: EC expire after 6 months (monitoring packs) or 12 months (portfolio pools) from purchase date, unless rolled over per contract terms.

**Q: Can I see EC usage in real-time?**  
A: Yes. Enterprise and Portfolio customers get real-time EC usage dashboards. You can set alerts when balance drops below custom thresholds.

---

### Technical Questions

**Q: What integrations are available for Digital Signal Evidence?**  
A: Currently: Energy meters (Victron, SMA, Huawei), GPS trackers (10+ providers), ERP systems (SAP, Odoo), mobile money (M-Pesa, MTN), government registries (varies by country). Custom integrations available for Enterprise.

**Q: How does the Local Lite Validator network work?**  
A: We maintain a vetted network of field verifiers in 35 countries. You request verification → we dispatch nearest LLV → they collect geotagged evidence → you receive verified report within 48-72 hours.

**Q: Can we self-host or get on-premise deployment?**  
A: Available for Government & Multilateral programs only. Contact our team for infrastructure requirements and pricing.

**Q: Do you have an API?**  
A: Yes. API access included in Enterprise tier and above. Full REST API with webhook support for real-time evidence submission and Trust Score updates.
